🛡️ Option A Margin Protection Guarantee:

Because upgrades are calculated as Base Package sellPrice + (Replacement Retail Price - Original Retail Price), the agency's initial bundle discount remains anchored to the base items and never inflates on luxury upgrades. When a customer swaps a ₹25,000 hotel for a ₹31,000 hotel, they pay the exact ₹6,000 difference, protecting the agency's ₹9,000 base gross margin!

📐 Option A Formula Reference:

  • Min-2 Guardrail: $\text{Active Offers} \ge 2$
  • Swap Delta: $\Delta = \text{Replacement Retail Price} - \text{Original Retail Price}$
  • Customized Final Total: $\text{Final Total} = \text{Base Package sellPrice} + \sum (\text{Add-On Offers}) - \sum (\text{Removed Offers}) + \sum \Delta$
  • Atomic Claim: Atomically claims physical seats in TicketStock and rooms in HotelStock.
